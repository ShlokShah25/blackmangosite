BLACK MANGO

Floating video & room try-on

Two additions for blackmangoobjects.com, made to drop into the current Shopify theme without changing anything else.

Floating video

Every product floating in a seamless 12-second loop. Five colour options, each in 16:9 (1920 × 1080) and 4:5 (1080 × 1350), as MP4 and WebM.

Room try-on

On any product page, "View in your room" opens the try-on with that piece selected.

  • Upload a room photo, which stays on the customer's device, or use the sample room.
  • Rugs lie on the floor, cushions sit on the sofa, pedestals stand on the floor, and glassware goes on a table.
  • Drag, pinch, resize, rotate or flip the piece, then add it to the cart or save the picture to share.

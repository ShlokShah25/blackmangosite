# Editorial product photos (concept comps)

Four directions in the aesthetic of Manasi's references: real raw places, daylight, rugs treated as art objects out of context. Each image is 1600 × 2000 (4:5).

| File | Direction | Pieces |
|---|---|---|
| `1-gallery-wall.jpg` | Rug hung on raw concrete like a canvas, raking sun | Flow rug |
| `2-sky-line.jpg` | Rugs and cushions pegged on yellow rope in a blue sky | Adrift and Fantasy rugs; Groove, Jolt and Zig cushions |
| `3-crossing.jpg` | Zebra rug on a zebra crossing, people passing in low sun | Balance rug |
| `4-rooftop.jpg` | Giant rug draped from the parapet of a brutalist block | Fit-In rug |

These are composites of the real product photos on free-licence Unsplash backgrounds. Use them as a direction to choose from. The final images should come from a real shoot.
